Privacy Policy

Postgame Studio · effective October 24, 2026

Who we are

Postgame Studio is the internal publishing tool of the Postgame YouTube channel (NBA recaps in Russian). It is operated by the channel owner, contact: krasilnikov.pavel@gmail.com.

YouTube API Services

Postgame Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

  • The Postgame channel's own YouTube data: its video list, and the videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and privacy status that the tool uploads or edits.
  • Public data of the official NBA YouTube channel (public video titles and durations) to find highlight clips.

The tool does not access data of any other user, does not read comments, subscribers or analytics of other channels, and does not collect personal information.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish and manage videos on the Postgame channel. The OAuth access token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page. After that the tool can no longer access the channel, and the stored token is deleted on request.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: krasilnikov.pavel@gmail.com.
