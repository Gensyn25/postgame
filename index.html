POSTGAME

Postgame is a YouTube channel with short morning recaps of NBA games in Russian: every game of the night, the top plays and the closest finish, with Russian commentary.

Postgame Studio

Postgame Studio is the channel's internal publishing tool. It is used only by the channel owner to:

  • upload finished recap videos to the Postgame YouTube channel as private videos;
  • set titles, descriptions, chapters and thumbnails of those videos;
  • make a video public after the owner approves it;
  • read the list of public videos of the official NBA channel to find highlight clips of each game.

The tool is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
